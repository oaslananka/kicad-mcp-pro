# Sponsor and partner with KiCad MCP Pro

**Status: seeking sponsors and technical collaborators.** This page describes opportunities, not existing endorsements. No named company is represented here as a sponsor unless support has been agreed and acknowledgement has been authorized.

[KiCad MCP Pro](https://github.com/oaslananka/kicad-mcp-pro) is an independent, MIT-licensed project that lets MCP-capable AI assistants inspect and assist with KiCad schematic, PCB, BOM, ERC/DRC, DFM and manufacturing-preparation workflows. Engineering outputs require human review; the project does not certify hardware for manufacture.

## Ways to support

- **Financial sponsorship:** help fund maintenance, reproducible testing, documentation, cross-platform packaging and community onboarding.
- **Engineering and data collaboration:** improve component sourcing, BOM workflows and traceable EDA integrations using documented, permissioned APIs.
- **Infrastructure and developer tooling:** contribute hosting, CI/testing capacity, code-signing or developer tooling, subject to the project's technical and licensing boundaries.
- **Demonstration and education:** jointly develop reproducible tutorials or real-board engineering examples that clearly report evidence, limitations and human-review requirements.

## Recognition options

Supporter, Corporate Sponsor and Strategic Technical Partner are **discussion categories**, not currently sold plans or promises of a particular impression count. Recognition levels, contribution terms, duration, logo use, and publication scope are agreed in writing before any logos or claims appear.

Possible recognition includes a verified public acknowledgement here, an approved linked logo, a sponsor credit in a dated release note, or a jointly reviewed technical case study. Neither visibility nor content placement guarantees sales, leads or downloads.

## Independence and transparency

- Sponsorship does not buy preferential technical conclusions, recommendation rankings, security exemptions, repository administration, or write access to users' designs.
- Component prices, stock, supplier selection and design quality remain evidence-based. A supplier integration is not a partnership endorsement.
- No exclusivity, paid cloud/API spend, production-data access or public logo usage is granted by this page.
- Proposals that require tracking users, accessing nonpublic designs or influencing safety/manufacturing sign-off are outside the default scope.
