    Engineering Review & Baseline Control Inventory (T0)¶

    Date: 2026-11-03 Milestone: M18 — Engineering Review & Visual Quality Issue: #1578 (T0 — Baseline Inventory) Reproduction Suite: tests/unit/test_engineering_review_baseline_false_pass.py

    Executive Summary¶

    This inventory maps all existing engineering controls, validation gates, critics, and review mechanisms across kicad-mcp-pro. In accordance with milestone M18 principles: 1. A tool name, rule name, report field, or checklist item is not proof that its underlying physical condition is verified. 2. Incomplete coverage, missing inputs, unsupported geometry, or stale evidence must be reported honestly (NOT_ASSESSED or BLOCKED) and must never silently adapt to PASS. 3. Readability and aesthetic style preferences are distinct from electrical correctness and manufacturing compliance.

    Classification Taxonomy¶

    • existing_verified: Evaluated directly by an authoritative engine (native KiCad CLI, exact S-expression parser with real coordinates, or complete mathematical formula).
    • existing_bounded: Evaluated with explicit, documented 2D or nominal limits; does not claim full 3D, physics, or fab approval.
    • integration_gap: Check exists or is claimed, but contains a bypass, missing net binding, missing pin coordinate, or keyword-presence proxy.
    • new_check: Proposed or declared in rules/docs/milestones but not currently implemented.
    • external_review: Requires external specialist review, physical lab test, or human signoff.

    1. Verified False-PASS Reproductions¶

    Six suspected false-PASS paths were statically identified and reproduced deterministically in test_engineering_review_baseline_false_pass.py:

    # Suspected Path Owning Service / Function Real Mechanism Reproduction Status
    1 Generic 90° corner treated as electrical defect src/kicad_mcp/tools/validation.py
    _evaluate_route_corner_style_gate()    		 Fails release gate unconditionally (max_90_degree_corners=0) even for low-speed DC or non-SI tracks where modern fabs have no issue. Reproduced (test_reproduce_generic_90_degree_style_treated_as_electrical_defect)
    2 Workflow completed metadata treated as evidence src/kicad_mcp/tools/project_workflow.py
    build_design_workflow()    		 Caller passes a list of phase names as strings; service marks them COMPLETE without evaluating ERC/DRC/evidence. Reproduced (test_reproduce_workflow_completed_metadata_treated_as_evidence)
    3 File/keyword presence treated as insulation review src/kicad_mcp/manufacturing/release_evidence.py
    _evaluate_hv_safety_gate()    		 Scans *.kicad_dru for substring "clearance". Any default clearance rule passes a 230V hazardous mains safety check. Reproduced (test_reproduce_hv_safety_keyword_presence_treated_as_insulation_review)
    4 Near but wrong-net capacitor src/kicad_mcp/tools/emc_compliance.py
    _nearest_cap_distance_mm()    		 Searches for any footprint starting with "C" within max_distance_mm of IC origin; completely ignores net connectivity (e.g. +12V cap passes for +3.3V IC). Reproduced (test_reproduce_near_wrong_net_capacitor_passes_decoupling)
    5 Missing power pin falling back to footprint centre src/kicad_mcp/tools/signal_integrity_decoupling_placement.py
    _find_power_anchor()    		 If named power_pin is not found on pads of ic_ref, it silently returns point_xy_mm(footprint.position) rather than raising or returning NOT_ASSESSED. Reproduced (test_reproduce_missing_power_pin_fallback_to_footprint_centre)
    6 Empty or unsupported results becoming PASS src/kicad_mcp/models/visual_qa.py
    run_cosmetic_qa()    		 A blank schematic with 0 symbols and 0 wires returns status="PASS" and cosmetic_score >= 95.0 because penalty loop has 0 findings. Reproduced (test_reproduce_empty_schematic_cosmetic_score_passes_without_circuit)

    The six tests intentionally reproduce pre-fix behavior as historical evidence. When a control is corrected, update its reproduction test to assert the repaired outcome and revise this dated inventory; do not preserve false-PASS behavior merely to keep a historical reproduction green.
